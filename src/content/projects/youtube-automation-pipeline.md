## The problem

Producing and publishing content on a regular schedule is repetitive. The goal was a pipeline that runs without manual steps, from idea to published post.

## Approach

- Built the whole system in **n8n** as multi-step workflows covering triggers, data transformation and cross-platform API calls.
- Integrated **OpenAI / OpenRouter** models for script and content generation.
- Automated publishing to **LinkedIn** through its API.
